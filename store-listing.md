# Store listing text

## Summary (Firefox: 250 chars; Chrome: 132 chars)

Keep a saved list of excluded words for Indeed job searches, without losing your pay, radius and other filters.

## Description

Indeed lets you exclude words from a job search by typing -"word" in the search box, but searching again resets your pay, radius and other filters.

This extension fixes that:

- Keep a list of excluded words or phrases. They're added to every search automatically.
- Add or remove a word from a small panel on the results page, and the search re-runs right away with all your filters kept.
- Searching from Indeed's own search box keeps your filters too.
- Exclusions already in a search link are added to your list.
- Your list syncs through your browser account.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Indeed.

## Category

Firefox: Search Tools. Chrome: Tools.

## Privacy

No data is collected or sent anywhere. The excluded-words list is stored in the browser's extension storage, which syncs through your own Firefox or Chrome account if sync is on.
